Privacy Policy

Poppin Internal Tools • Effective Date: June 6, 2026

At Poppin, we believe that transparency is the foundation of trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information managed across our internal tools and applications, in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy applies to all employees and contractors accessing our internal ecosystem.

1. Commitment to Privacy

We are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data security. Our internal ecosystem is built with a "Privacy-First" architecture, ensuring that team data is encrypted, secure, and accessible only by authorized personnel. We regularly review our internal policies to ensure they remain consistent with evolving data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

To ensure our internal tools function efficiently, we process the following types of information:

  • Authentication Data: Identity information used to verify access, including corporate email addresses and secure passkey identifiers.
  • Operational Data: Content and inputs provided within our internal applications (e.g., transaction records, project updates, or internal logs).
  • System Metadata: Information regarding user activity, including timestamps, device identifiers, and audit logs to ensure system integrity and accountability.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary to provide you with access to tools required for your employment.
  • Legitimate Interests: We have a legitimate interest in maintaining secure, efficient, and auditable internal systems.
  • Legal Obligation: We may be required to process information to comply with mandatory record-keeping requirements under UK law.

4. Use of Third-Party Infrastructure

We utilize Supabase as our backend infrastructure partner to provide secure database and authentication services. We have carefully vetted their compliance standards. As part of our commitment to transparency, we encourage all users to review the Supabase Privacy Policy to understand how they handle data within the infrastructure they provide.

5. Use of Information

Information processed within Poppin Internal Tools is used exclusively for internal operations, including:

  • Maintaining accurate and up-to-date internal records.
  • Providing historical audit trails to ensure corporate transparency and accountability.
  • Optimizing system performance, troubleshooting technical issues, and enhancing user experience.

We do not sell, rent, or disclose this information to third parties for any purpose outside of our core operational needs.

6. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted is protected using HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Access Control: Access is restricted strictly to authenticated staff members through role-based access control (RBAC).
  • Infrastructure Security: Databases are governed by robust, row-level security policies, ensuring users can only interact with data relevant to their role.
  • Employee Awareness: We provide periodic internal guidance regarding data hygiene and security best practices.

7. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill our operational and audit requirements. Under UK legislation, you have the right to:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the data associated with your account.
  • Right of Rectification: Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete entries.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of data, subject to our overriding legal or internal audit obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

8. International Data Transfers

Our primary servers are hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. As this location is within the European Economic Area (EEA), it benefits from the UK's adequacy regulations, ensuring that the high standards of data protection required by the UK GDPR are maintained. For other service providers that may operate servers outside of the UK or EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards, such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), are in place to maintain the protections afforded by the UK GDPR.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Significant updates will be communicated to the team through our standard internal channels, and the "Effective Date" at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or wish to exercise any of your rights regarding your data, please contact the IT Security Team or the designated Poppin Data Privacy Officer.
