Resolution Portal

Statement of Commitment

Poppin is committed to identifying and resolving all outstanding payroll obligations for former team members of ChocoLuxe. We recognize that errors in compensation are serious and we have established this portal to ensure that every affected individual receives the payment they are owed in a timely and secure manner.

How to Submit a Claim

1. Gather Required Documentation

3. Verification & Resolution

What to Expect

Your personal information is handled with the utmost confidentiality. All data submitted through this portal is encrypted. We will never ask for your bank password or sensitive login credentials.
