About Poppin

Crafted with Passion

Poppin was created by three people who had a passion for popcorn. We want to share that passion with you. Poppin uses 100% natural ingredients, and we offer UK wide delivery.

What started as three friends experimenting with popcorn recipes in our kitchens has grown into a mission to bring the perfect popcorn experience to everyone. We believe that great popcorn should be simple, natural, and absolutely delicious. That's why we spend countless hours testing different kernels, perfecting our seasoning blends, and refining our popping techniques until every batch is just right.

Our journey began when we realized that most popcorn available was either too processed, loaded with artificial ingredients, or just plain boring. We knew there had to be a better way. Through trial, error, and lots of taste testing (the best part!), we developed our signature flavors that celebrate the natural goodness of popcorn while adding exciting twists that keep you coming back for more.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Royal Mail 2nd Class typically takes 2-3 working days, while Next Day delivery arrives the next working day if ordered before 2pm.

How long does the popcorn stay fresh?

Our popcorn stays fresh for up to 2 weeks when stored in an airtight container. For best taste, we recommend enjoying within 7 days.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently we ship within the UK only. We're working on expanding our delivery zones soon!

Are your ingredients allergen-free?

Our Original flavor contains dairy. Please check individual product descriptions for full allergen information.
